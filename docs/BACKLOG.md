  - PE: 100-day plan, exit options.
  - VC: cohort & unit-economics diligence, Series B follow-on.
  - Corp Dev: target screening & build/buy/partner, divestiture with TSA.

  Rank-specific missions above the first promotion are still to come. - Cross-career switching is playable once per timeline, after the first-year review. Chị Ngân (headhunter, Khu Tài chính) offers 8 paths: IB→PE, IB→CD, CS→CD, CS→PE, CD→PE, CD→IM, VC→PE and PE→VC. Each has a transferability analysis, a technical interview aimed at the gap, a behavioural interview, a professional resignation and an offer trade-off (lower rank, keep 70% reputation). A failed or declined attempt can be retried and grants nothing. A post-switch review promotes based on the new chain. Not yet: VC→Startup Operator, PE→Family Office, more than one switch per timeline.
- The Keyrus Deal Academy tracks 12 deals, but only Vuealta is playable. The other 11 are marked Pending Research. inlumi is verified from its public release but has no mission yet.
- No daily or weekly quests, seasonal events or Supabase sync.
- Disney/Pixar and RJR source links point to EDGAR search, the IR page and an archive search, not to specific filings. Pin exact filing URLs once they are verified.
- Apple touch icon is SVG. iOS prefers a 180×180 PNG, so add one for the home-screen icon.
- Leaving a mission midway discards its progress by design. There is no mid-mission save.

AI disruption in year 4 offers: transform, sell to a strategic buyer, or ignore (−10%/yr). Calibration shows: the disciplined fund is Q2 without plans and Q1 with them, and operating partners help further. LP-funded paydown beats a NAV loan, which beats expensive refinancing; AI transformation beats selling, which beats ignoring on IRR.
